# Sales-Dashboard
## 1.	Project Title / Headline
### 📊 Sales Insights Dashboard: 
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, track revenue trends, and uncover actionable business insights across products, regions, and time periods.

### 2. Short Description / Purpose
The Sales Insights Dashboard is a dynamic and visually engaging Power BI report that helps businesses monitor key sales metrics, identify growth opportunities, and make data-driven decisions. It enables users to explore revenue patterns, customer trends, and product performance efficiently.

## 3. Tech Stack
The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:<br>
• 📊 Power BI Desktop – Main platform used to design and develop the interactive dashboard.<br>
• 📂 Power Query – Used for data cleaning, transformation, and shaping.<br>
• 🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used to create calculated measures like total sales, profit, and growth rates.<br>
• 🔗 Data Modeling – Established relationships between tables such as sales, customers, products, and regions.<br>
• 📁 File Format – .pbix for development and .png for dashboard preview.<br>

## 4. Data Source
The dataset includes:<br>
• Sales transactions (order ID, date, revenue, profit)<br>
• Product details (category, sub-category)<br>
• Customer information (segment, location)<br>
• Regional data (country/state/city)<br>

## 5. Features / Highlights
• Business Problem
 Organizations generate large volumes of sales data, but without proper visualization tools, it becomes difficult to:<br>
• Track overall performance<br>
• Identify top-performing products<br>
• Understand regional sales trends<br>
• Detect declining sales areas<br>
• Make quick strategic decisions<br>

• Goal of the Dashboard<br>
 To build an interactive dashboard that:<br>
• Provides a clear overview of sales performance<br>
• Helps identify trends and patterns in revenue and profit<br>
• Enables comparison across regions, products, and time<br>
• Supports business decision-making with data insights<br>

## • Walkthrough of Key Visuals
🔹 Key KPIs (Top Section)<br>
• Total Sales<br>
• Total Profit<br>
• Total Orders<br>
• Profit Margin (%)<br>

👉 Gives a quick snapshot of overall business performance.

🔹 Sales Trend Over Time (Line Chart)

Displays sales performance across months/years.
👉 Helps identify seasonality and growth trends.

🔹 Sales by Region (Map / Bar Chart)

Visual representation of sales across different locations.
👉 Helps identify high-performing and low-performing regions.

🔹 Top Products / Categories (Bar Chart)

Ranks products or categories based on revenue or profit.
👉 Highlights best-selling and most profitable items.

🔹 Customer Segmentation (Pie / Donut Chart)

Breakdown of customers by segment (e.g., Consumer, Corporate).
👉 Helps understand target audience and contribution.

🔹 Profit vs Sales Analysis (Scatter / Bar Chart)

Compares profitability across products or regions.
👉 Identifies high-revenue but low-profit areas.

🔹 Filters / Slicers Panel

Includes filters such as:
• Date range
• Region
• Product category

👉 Enables interactive and customized analysis.

• Business Impact & Insights

📈 Improved Decision Making
Managers can quickly assess performance and take data-driven actions.

📊 Revenue Optimization
Identify top-performing products and focus on high-profit areas.

🌍 Regional Strategy
Understand which regions need improvement or expansion.

👥 Customer Insights
Analyze customer segments to improve targeting and marketing.

📉 Problem Identification
Detect declining sales trends and take corrective measures early.
